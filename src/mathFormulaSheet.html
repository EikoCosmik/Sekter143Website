Useful Mathematic Symbols

Communicational:

  • Because: $\because$
  • Therefore: $\therefore$

Functional:

  • Add / Plus: $+$
  • Subtract / Minus: $-$
  • Multiply / Times: $\times$
  • Divide: $\div$

Logical:

  • AND: $p\wedge y$ (if $p$ and $q$)
  • NAND: $p\uparrow y$ (if ($p$ and not $q$) or (not $p$ and $q$) or Neither)
  • OR: $p\vee y$ (if $p$ or $q$ or Both)
  • NOR $p\downarrow y$ (if not $p$ and not $q$)
  • XOR: $p\oplus y$ (if $p$ or $q$ and not Both)
  • XNOR: $p\leftrightarrow y$ (if Both or Neither)
  • Implication: $p\rightarrow y$ (if $p$ then $q$)

Proof:

  • For All: $\forall$

Basic Operations

Addition:

  • $2+3=2+1+1+1$
  • $2+3=3+2$
  • $2+3=5$

Subtraction:

  • $2-3=2-1-1-1$
  • $2-3=2+-3=-3+2$
  • $2-3=-1$

Multiplication:

  • $2\times3=2+2+2$
  • $2\times3=3\times2$
  • $2\times3=6$

Division:

  • $2\div3=\frac{2}{3}$
  • $2\div3=2\times\frac{1}{3}$
  • $2\div3=0.\overline{6}$

Powers / Exponentiation:

  • $2^2=2\text{^}2$
  • $2^2=2\times2$
  • $2^3=2\times2\times2$

Roots / Radicals:

  • $\sqrt[n]{x}=x^\frac{1}{n}$
  • $\sqrt[2]{x}=\sqrt{x}$
  • $\sqrt{4}=2$

Fraction / Decimal / Percentage Help:

  • Whatever is done to the top of a fraction must be done to the bottom of the fraction to maintain equivalance.
  • $\frac{1}{4}=\frac{25}{100}\because\frac{100}{4}=25\therefore\frac{1*25}{4*25}=\frac{25}{100}$
  • $\frac{25}{100}=0.25$
  • $\frac{25}{100}=25\%\therefore25\%=0.25$
  • In general, fractional answers are prefered over decimal answers

Divisibility Rules:

  • If $x$ is a number
  • If the last digit of $x$ is Even
  • If the sum of the digits of $x$ is divisible by 3 (Repeatable)
  • If the last 2 digits of $x$ are divisible by 4
  • If the last digit of $x$ is 5 OR 0
  • If $x$ is divisible by 2 AND 3
  • When taking the last digit of $x$, Doubling it and subtracting the result from the remaining of the digits of $x$. If the result is divisible by 7 (Repeatable)$175\rightarrow17-(2\times5)=7$
  • If the last 3 digits of $x$ are divisible by 8
  • If the sum of the digits of $x$ is divisible by 9 (Repeatable)
  • If the last digit of $x$ is 0
  • When subtracting the last digit of $x$ form the remainging digits of $x$. If the result is divisible by 11 (Repeatable)$121\rightarrow12-1=11$
  • When taking the last digit of $x$, Multiplying by 4 and adding the result from the remainging digits of $x$, If the result is divisible by 13 (Repeatable)$52\rightarrow5+(2\times4)=13$
  • When taking the last digit of $x$, Multiplying by 5 and subtracting the result from the remainging digits of $x$, If the result is divisible by 17 (Repeatable)$34\rightarrow3-(4\times5)=-17$
  • When taking the last digit of $x$, Multiplying by 2 and adding the result from the remainging digits of $x$, If the result is divisible by 19 (Repeatable)$95\rightarrow9+(5\times2)=19$

Polynomials

Identites:

  • $(u-v)^2 = u^2-2uv+v^2$
  • $(u+v)^2 = u^2+2uv+v^2$
  • $(u+v)(u-v) = u^2-v^2$

Factoring:

  • $2x^2+4x=2x(x+2)$
  • $3x^2+6x+4x+8=3x(x+2)+4(x+2)=(x+2)(3x+4)$
